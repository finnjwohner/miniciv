# Barbarian Camps are not Nations

Barbarian Camps have an Army, defensive Buildings and Warparties, and they can be Located and attacked, so it is tempting to model them as Nations no Player leads. We decided against that: a Camp is its own small model that reuses Warparties, Battles and Encounters, and Locating, Warparty targets and Encounters are widened to accept "a Nation or a Barbarian Camp". A Nation carries Gold, Population, Research, Technologies, Spies, Tasks, Events, Dilemmas and Turn Reports. Treating Camps as Nations would mean guarding every rule that walks all Nations (upkeep, growth, Events, Dilemmas, Spies, Tribute, Messages, the last Nation standing) with a "not a Camp" exception, and each missed guard would be a bug, such as a plague hitting a Camp or a Camp winning the Game.
